- Leaves: the current `/en/shop/coffee` catalog belongs to the same Shopify merchant as `shop.leavescoffee.jp`. - Luminous: exhaust Dropstore and Lumi XL without scanning the archive. Keep the sold-out instant box, partial packaging-option stock, and sold-out tier.
